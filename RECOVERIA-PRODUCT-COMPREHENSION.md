# RecoverIA Product Comprehension

## Five-minute operator journey

1. Open Inicio and confirm the tenant and synthetic mode.
2. Read outstanding and overdue totals, then verify that aging buckets explain the portfolio composition.
3. Open the first Attention-of-Today item and inspect its priority, reasons, blockers, and evidence.
4. Follow the entity link to understand administration-level concentration and available contacts.
5. Open a related invoice to distinguish issued amount, pending amount, due date, and review state.
6. Visit Importaciones to see that parsing success, review-required, unsupported, and failed states are separate outcomes.

## What the product claims

RecoverIA can organize synthetic receivable evidence, calculate current balances and aging deterministically, surface explainable priorities, and expose uncertain imports for human review.

## What the product does not claim

It does not prove collectability, determine legal strategy, confirm ambiguous identity automatically, guarantee parser correctness, or act externally. Phase 4.7 demonstrates the product model, not production readiness or Client Zero ingestion.

## Interpretation guide

“Pending” is a reconciled ledger balance. “Overdue” is date-based. “Review” means evidence is insufficient or conflicting. “Priority” is a deterministic ordering under the documented Phase 4 policy. “Evidence” identifies the synthetic record that supports the displayed reason; it is not a legal conclusion.
