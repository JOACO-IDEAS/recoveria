# RecoverIA Phase 4 Operational Intelligence

Phase 4 adds pure tenant-scoped portfolio and attention services. They calculate outstanding totals, administration/building/party concentration, largest/oldest items, multiple overdue invoices, missing contacts, unresolved evidence, missed promises, disputes, stale cases, and configured legal-review flags.

The engine recommends work only; it performs no action. Every reason/blocker carries fixture evidence references. Safety states precede routine collection: legal review, unsafe identity/evidence, missing contact, dispute, missed promise, active promise, stale contact, old receivable, ordinary overdue, then no action. Results are bounded and deterministically ordered by tier, age, balance, and case ID.

Phase 4 intentionally leaves the UI at its minimal Phase 0 shell. Correctness and domain contracts are complete; no Agent, communications, OCR, infrastructure, or real data were added.
